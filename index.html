What it does

Six modules. One honest dashboard.

Every module writes to the same local store, so the heatmap and dashboard reflect exactly what actually happened — not what you meant to do.

Module 01

Habits

Weekly habit tracking, anchored to Monday. Each habit rolls over on schedule automatically, so a habit you check off on Sunday doesn't quietly bleed into next week.

Module 02

Todos

Prioritized, day-to-day tasks that sit next to your habits instead of buried in a separate app. Set what matters most and clear it off the list.

Module 03

Water

Quick ml-preset logging and OS-level reminders that anchor to your first drink of the day, then space themselves out until you hit your goal.

Module 04

Journal

A plain daily entry for the things a checkbox can't capture. Titled, timestamped, and stored alongside everything else that happened that day.

Module 05

Dashboard

Charts built from your real logs — completion rates, water trends, streak history — so progress is something you can see, not something you assume.

Module 06

Activity heatmap

A calendar-style grid, color-coded by how much of the day you actually finished, with the specific tasks from any day on tap. No day gets rewritten.

Philosophy

Simple tracking.
Meaningful progress.
No self-deception.

Most tracking apps are optimized to make you feel good about your data. Daily Tracker is optimized to make your data accurate — a completed habit looks different from an ignored one, a partial day looks different from a perfect one, and the heatmap never smooths that over.

It's a solo, local-first Android project: everything is stored on your device with shared preferences, there's no account to create and nothing to sync to a server. The tradeoff is real — you own the data and the honesty that comes with it.

It's built with Flutter, iterated on in focused sessions, and shipped as an open Android project for anyone who wants the same kind of honest log for their own days.
